This notice explains how CleanOps Connect uses personal data about customers, cleaners and visitors to this website, as required by Articles 13 and 14 of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect

  • Account details — name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a one-way hash) and account type.
  • Job details (customers) — postcode, the location derived from it, service type, description, budget, frequency and job history.
  • Cleaner profile — name, trading or company name, base postcode, coverage area, services, availability, rates, contact details you choose to add, ratings, and compliance status (DBS, COSHH training, insurance).
  • Compliance documents (cleaners) — the DBS certificate, COSHH training certificate and insurance certificate you choose to upload, so our team can check them before a badge appears on your profile. Only our admin staff can open them, and each time one is opened it is recorded in an audit log.
  • Job photos (optional) — before and after pictures a cleaner chooses to add to a job, and pictures a customer chooses to add when reporting a problem. A picture can show the inside of a home and anyone or anything in it, so please photograph only the work, and avoid people and personal papers. The customer, the cleaner on that job and our staff can see them; each time our staff open one it is recorded in an audit log.
  • Payments — card payments are taken by Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers. We keep payment references, amounts, refunds and payout status.
  • Disputes and support — the reason code and any detail you give when raising a dispute, and records of how it was resolved.
  • Messages — messages you send in the in-app chat with the customer or cleaner on a job. Phone numbers, email addresses, postcodes and links are hidden before a message is saved, and we keep only the hidden version plus a note that something was hidden. Our staff can read a conversation only to resolve a dispute or investigate misuse; each time they do, the reason is recorded in an audit log.
  • Reviews — the rating and comment you leave after a completed job. Reviews are published with your first name and last initial only.
  • Cleaner payouts — Stripe verifies cleaners' identity and bank details during payout set-up. We only keep the Stripe account reference and whether payouts are enabled.
  • Technical data — IP address and request logs needed to run and secure the service.

If you upload a DBS certificate, the copy is stored in our database, can be opened only by our admin staff, and is deleted automatically 6 months after we check it (or 6 months after upload if it has not been checked); we keep the record that it was checked and the decision. Our criminal offence data policy explains how we protect it.

Automated matching

When a customer asks us to find cleaners, we rank eligible cleaners using a fixed set of weighted rules: distance, experience, rating, insurance and compliance status. The top-ranked cleaners are sent an offer. Cleaners decide whether to accept and set their own price. The ranking does not decide whether anyone gets a job on its own, and you can contact us to ask how it was applied to you.

Who we share it with

  • The other party to a job — once a job is offered or booked, the cleaner sees the job details they need to do it. Photos added to a job are seen by both sides of it.
  • Stripe — payment processing, holding funds and cleaner payouts.
  • Mapbox — turning postcodes into map locations.
  • Anthropic — only if you press "Help me write this" on the job form or your cleaner profile: the job type, frequency, estimated hours, or the services, travel distance and bio text you entered, plus any text you typed, are sent to suggest wording. Please don't include names, contact details or your address. It is also used, with web search, if you ask for a local price guide: only the service type and the first part of a postcode (for example LS12, never the full postcode) are sent.
  • Resend — sending emails about your bookings.
  • Hosting providers — DigitalOcean (our application and our database, both in the London region) and Cloudflare (website delivery).
  • Regulators, the police or courts where the law requires it.

Our database is hosted in the UK (London). DigitalOcean, Cloudflare, Stripe, Anthropic and Mapbox are US companies, so some of the data they handle may be processed in the United States. Where that happens we rely on the UK–US data bridge for providers certified under it and, for any that are not, on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data — while your account is open. If you ask us to close it, we deal with the request under “Your rights” below and keep only what the law requires, such as payment records (below).
  • Compliance documents — DBS certificate copies are deleted 6 months after we check them; a file we reject is deleted after 30 days; a COSHH or insurance certificate is kept while it is the one in use, and deleted when you replace it and the new one is approved. The record of the check (who, when, and the decision) is kept with your profile.
  • Job photos — deleted 90 days after the job is closed (confirmed, cancelled, or any reported problem settled), and in every case within 12 months of being added. You can remove a photo you added yourself until a problem is reported on the job.
  • Payment and transaction records — 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, to meet HMRC record-keeping rules.
  • Chat messages — deleted automatically 6 months after they are sent.
  • Dispute records — the written description of a problem is deleted 12 months after it was reported. The reason code and the outcome stay with the payment record (above).
  • Security logs, including the record of which staff opened or changed what — deleted automatically after 12 months.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected, have it erased, restrict or object to how we use it, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
