Automated matching

When a customer asks us to find cleaners, we rank eligible cleaners using a fixed set of weighted rules: distance, experience, rating, insurance and compliance status. The top-ranked cleaners are sent an offer. Cleaners decide whether to accept and set their own price. The ranking does not decide whether anyone gets a job on its own, and you can contact us to ask how it was applied to you.
