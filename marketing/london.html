Cleaners in London

CleanOps Connect is an online marketplace. We match people in London who need cleaning with independent cleaners who cover their area, and we handle the payment. The cleaning itself is done by the cleaner, who is self-employed or runs their own business.

What you can book

  • Domestic cleaning for your home.
  • Commercial and industrial cleaning, which you can put out to tender.

How it works in London

  1. Post a job with your London postcode (E, EC, N, NW, SE, SW, W or WC) and what you need done.
  2. Cleaners who have set their coverage to include your postcode can offer to do it. Each cleaner sets their own price.
  3. You choose an offer and pay by card through Stripe. We hold the money until the job is done.
  4. After the cleaner marks the job complete you have 48 hours to confirm it or raise a dispute. If you do neither, the job is confirmed and the cleaner is paid.

What we check

Cleaners add evidence of their DBS check, COSHH training and insurance. A badge appears on a cleaner's profile only after our team has checked that evidence. How many cleaners are available in London at any time depends on who has signed up and set their coverage there.

Prices

We do not set prices. Cleaners set their own, and we never take a fee on top of it from the customer. Our commission comes out of the cleaner's price. We may show a guide to typical local rates, which is a suggestion only. The terms of use explain this in full.

Are you a cleaner in London?

Register as a cleaner, add your documents and set the areas you cover to see job offers from London customers. Cleaners pay a commission on each job, described in the terms, and a small platform fee.
