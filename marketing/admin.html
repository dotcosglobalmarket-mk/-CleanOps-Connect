Payments Ops Queue

Jobs stuck in MANUAL_REVIEW_HOLD (exhausted the automatic transfer retry backoff) or PAYOUT_BLOCKED (the cleaner hasn't finished Stripe payouts onboarding — this clears itself once they do). Never triggers a customer refund; funds remain in the platform balance until manually resolved.

Cleaner Verification

DBS/COSHH/insurance are CleanOps's own compliance checks (separate from Stripe's onboarding KYC). Suspending a cleaner always routes through Under review first — there is no fully automated, irreversible deactivation.
