Payments Ops Queue

Jobs stuck in MANUAL_REVIEW_HOLD (exhausted the automatic transfer retry backoff) or PAYOUT_BLOCKED (the cleaner hasn't finished Stripe payouts onboarding — this clears itself once they do). Never triggers a customer refund; funds remain in the platform balance until manually resolved.

Cleaner Verification

DBS/COSHH/insurance are CleanOps's own compliance checks (separate from Stripe's onboarding KYC). Suspending a cleaner always routes through Under review first — there is no fully automated, irreversible deactivation.

Document Review

Certificates cleaners have uploaded for their DBS, COSHH and insurance badges. Approving one switches on that badge; insurance needs the date it runs out. Opening a file is recorded in the audit log. DBS copies are deleted 6 months after the decision, rejected files after 30 days.

Refund Approvals

Dispute refunds that an operator resolved above the ops refund limit wait here for an admin decision. Approving issues the refund through Stripe straight away. Rejecting leaves the job disputed so it can be resolved again. You cannot decide a request you raised yourself.

Audit Log

Every action taken by staff, newest first (up to 200 entries). Entries cannot be edited or deleted.

Staff Accounts

Operators handle disputes, cleaner verification and the payments queue. They cannot suspend cleaners, approve refunds above the limit, manage staff or read the full audit log. Deactivating an account takes effect on the next request.
