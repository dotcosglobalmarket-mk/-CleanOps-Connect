Cleaners in UK cities

CleanOps Connect is an online marketplace for cleaning in the UK. We match each job with independent cleaners who have set their coverage to include the job's postcode, and we handle the payment. The cleaning itself is done by the cleaner, who is self-employed or runs their own business.

We do not have cleaners in every city. How many are available in your area depends on who has signed up and set their coverage there, and it changes over time. Enter your postcode when you post a job and we will offer it to the cleaners who cover it.

Are you a cleaner?

Register as a cleaner, add your documents and set the areas you cover to see job offers from customers there. Cleaners pay a commission on each job, described in the terms, and a small platform fee.

The 75 UK cities below are listed with their postcode area letters, as a guide to finding your postcode. Some cities span more than one area and some areas span more than one city. If your town is not listed, you can still enter your postcode: matching depends on cleaners' coverage, not on city status.
