FAQ

Got questions? We’ve got answers

CleanOps Connect converts your postcode to coordinates, keeps cleaners who offer the service, cover your area and are available (and insured, for industrial or higher-value jobs), then ranks them with a weighted score based on distance, experience, rating, insurance and compliance.

Our team checks each cleaner's DBS certificate, COSHH training and insurance before the matching badge appears on their profile. Badges are earned, never self-declared. Verified cleaners rank higher in matching, and only insured cleaners are offered industrial or higher-value jobs.

Register as a customer, then create a job with your postcode, budget, frequency, and service type (domestic deep clean, office clean, end of tenancy and more). We'll run allocation and send offers to matching cleaners.

The customer pays by card when the job is booked. Stripe holds the payment until the job is confirmed, then releases it to the cleaner minus our commission. This protects both sides.

Posting a job as a customer is free. Cleaners subscribe to a plan to receive job offers.
