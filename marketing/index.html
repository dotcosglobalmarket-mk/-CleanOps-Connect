Frequently asked questions

How does matching work?

CleanOps Connect converts your postcode to coordinates, filters cleaners by coverage area and compliance, then ranks the remaining candidates with a weighted AI score (distance, experience, rating, price fit, insurance, and compliance).

Are cleaners verified?

Domestic cleaners are DBS-checked and industrial cleaners carry COSHH training flags. Insurance status is validated before high-risk jobs are offered.

How do I post a job?

Register as a customer, then create a job with your postcode, budget, frequency, and service type. We'll run allocation and send offers to matching cleaners.

How do cleaners get paid?

Cleaners run monthly subscription tiers (Starter, Pro, Industrial Pro) with an optional insurance add-on, billed independently of individual jobs.

Is CleanOps Connect free?

Posting a job as a customer is free. Cleaners subscribe to a plan to receive job offers.
